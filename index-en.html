Welcome to a Special Place

Discover the tranquility and beauty of Valle Calchaquí. Our lodge is located at 2,300 meters above sea level in Ampimpa, Tucumán, surrounded by majestic landscapes.

We warmly welcome you to our little paradise, where nature, clear skies and pure air take center stage. Here you can disconnect from everyday life and reconnect with nature.

Our lodge offers a unique experience: from rustic circular cabañas to camping spaces under centuries-old algarrobo trees. Located near Route 307 and not far from the famous Route 40.

Ideal for excursions and outdoor relaxation – pool and starry nights included.

Nearby Attractions

Route 40

We are close to the legendary Route 40, surrounded by charming small towns. Each one with its own history, handicrafts and traditions that take you to the authentic Argentine north.

Pachamama Museum (8 km)

Immerse yourself in the region's pre-Columbian culture. Fascinating exhibitions of the indigenous peoples of Valle Calchaquí.

Sacred City of Quilmes (30 km)

One of Argentina's most important archaeological sites. The well-preserved ruins tell the story of the Quilmes people's resistance.

Ampimpa Observatory (8.5 km)

At 2,560 meters altitude is a renowned astronomical observatory, a popular destination for astrotourism.

Laguna de los Amaicheños

A high site in the Cumbres Calchaquíes, traditionally considered a mystical place of shamans. Pure nature and ancestral spirituality.

Cafayate (75 km)

The wine capital of the north! Charming colonial town, tastings at renowned wineries and the famous Torrontés wines of the region.

Location

How to find us: Salas Km 3.5, Ruta 307, km 107, Ampimpa, Tucumán

🚗 By Car

From Route 307 it is 3.5 km of gravel road to our lodge. We recommend your own vehicle to explore the region with flexibility.

🚌 Without a Car?

No problem! We offer a transfer service from Amaicha del Valle (8 km away) for an additional fee. Contact us before your arrival.

🛒 Important Note

Please bring your supplies! The nearest shop is in Ampimpa (4 km) or Amaicha del Valle (8 km) from the lodge. This way you can enjoy the tranquility of nature without interruptions.

FAQ

How do you get there?

At Ruta 307, km 107, a gravel road branches off toward Salas — passable for any car, though heavy summer rains can occasionally make it a bit harder. The gravel road leads to our turnoff, marked with red-painted stones bearing our logo — from there it's another 400 m of gravel down to the lodge. For reference: Tucumán airport approx. 3.5 hrs, Salta approx. 4 hrs, Tafí del Valle and Cafayate approx. 1 hr each.

Is there wifi?

Yes, in the bungalows, by the pool, and practically throughout the property.

The lodge runs on solar power — do guests need to limit anything?

Hardly. Wifi and electricity are available at all times, and hot water is guaranteed 24/7 thanks to our solar water heaters. We only ask guests not to plug in their own appliances such as irons, kettles, fridges, or hair dryers, as this would exceed the solar system's capacity.

What accommodation options are there?

Three cabañas in total: a Family Cabaña (up to 4 people) with private bathroom and kitchen, a cabaña with a double bed (up to 2 people) with bathroom and kitchen, and another cabaña with a double bed (up to 2 people) with bathroom and kitchen in the quincho (a covered communal barbecue area). We also have a room (up to 4 people) with four single beds and a bathroom — the kitchen for this room is in the quincho. Space available for camping, motorhomes and campervans — we only charge per person, with no extra fee for the tent or vehicle itself.

Are pets allowed?

Please check with us in advance. We have three cats of our own; leashed and always supervised dogs are welcome.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3:00 PM, check-out by 11:00 AM. A later check-out is available for a small extra charge, provided the bungalow hasn't already been booked for another guest — details are posted in each bungalow.

Is smoking allowed?

Not inside the cabañas; smoking is fine outdoors.

What is there to do nearby?

Close to us: the Quilmes ruins (5 km from Ruta 40), the observatory in Ampimpa, Tafí del Valle, Amaicha del Valle, the wine town of Cafayate in Salta, and Santa María in Catamarca. There are no marked hiking trails, but the surroundings invite endless free-form walking.

Is there a pool?

Yes, we have a pool available to all guests.
